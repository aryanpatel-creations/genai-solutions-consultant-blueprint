# Discovery: Operational Interview Guide (interview_guide.md)

> **Client:** Kestrel Bank  
> **Engagement:** AI Transformation Blueprint — Complaints Redressal Modernisation  
> **Prepared by:** Aryan Patel (MBA Business Analytics)  
> **Deliverable:** Flagship Step 2b — 30-Minute Semi-Structured Interview Instrument  
> **Target Participants:** Bank Operations Managers, Nodal Officers, Customer Service Leads, Frontline Dispute Agents

---

## 1. Interview Protocol & Ethics Guidelines

* **Confidentiality Notice:** Reassure the participant that this is an operational research initiative. No personally identifiable information (PII), customer account numbers, or proprietary core banking credentials will be collected or stored.
* **Objective:** Map operational reality versus documented SOPs, identify manual friction points, validate quantitative baselines (AHT, backlog lag, re-route frequency), and uncover organizational sentiment regarding AI automation.
* **Duration:** Exactly 30 minutes.

---

## 2. Semi-Structured Question Script

### Section 1: Warm-Up & Role Context (3 Minutes)
1. *"What is your current role and tenure in complaints operations or customer grievance redressal?"*
2. *"How does a typical complaint reach your desk or queue (CRM ticket, nodal email, branch escalation, RBI portal)?"*
3. *"Which banking products or operational queues do you primarily interact with on a daily basis?"*

### Section 2: Process Walkthrough & Hand-Offs (10 Minutes)
4. *"Walk me through the very last complex complaint you resolved, from intake to closure. What were the specific sequential steps?"*
5. *"Where in that lifecycle did you have to wait for someone else (supervisor approval, branch document, core banking log)?"*
6. *"How many different applications, screens, or PDF documents did you open to gather the facts for that single complaint?"*
7. *"How frequently are complaints initially routed to the wrong team, and what happens when you receive a misdirected ticket?"*

### Section 3: Operational Baselines & Quantitative Anchors (7 Minutes)
8. *"On average, how many minutes does an agent spend handling a typical complaint from start to finish? (Active touch time vs. elapsed turnaround time)?"*
9. *"Roughly what percentage of cases exceed the 30-day statutory resolution timeline under RB-IOS?"*
10. *"What percentage of closed complaints get reopened or escalated by dissatisfied customers?"*
11. *"Which 2 or 3 complaint issues take up the most investigative time and mental energy?"*

### Section 4: Operational Pain Points & AI Perceptions (7 Minutes)
12. *"If you could wave a magic wand and fix exactly one administrative headache in your daily workflow today, what would it be?"*
13. *"If you had an intelligent tool that instantly retrieved the exact RBI circular section or bank policy clause, how much time would that save you?"*
14. *"Where would you **NOT** trust an AI suggestion under any circumstances? What decisions must strictly remain 100% human?"*
15. *"How do you feel about the bank introducing AI tools? What are the biggest concerns among frontline staff?"*

### Section 5: Wrap-Up & Snowball Referrals (3 Minutes)
16. *"What critical aspect of complaint handling have I not asked about that an executive needs to understand?"*
17. *"Who else in operations, compliance, or IT architecture should I speak with to get the full picture?"*
