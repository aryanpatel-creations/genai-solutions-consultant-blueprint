| **Q7** | **How did you prioritise use cases across complaint handling?** | M6 (Flagship A) | Using the **AI Decision Ladder** (Process Fix $\to$ Rules $\to$ Classical ML $\to$ LLM/RAG $\to$ Agent), stopping at the cheapest viable rung. Triage (26/30) and Policy Q&A (24/30) are **Do First**; Draft Reply (22/30) is **Plan Carefully**; Auto-Send (17/30) and AI Compensation (14/30) were **strictly rejected (Risk = 1)**. | `04_FlagshipA/prioritisation/use_case_prioritisation.md` |
| **Q8** | **Why didn't you recommend a fully autonomous agent (`[AG]`) for customer replies?** | M6 (Flagship A) | Under our Decision Ladder and RBI FREE-AI guidelines, autonomous external customer communication carries fatal legal and regulatory liabilities. Autonomous agents add massive risk without solving the found bottleneck (which is policy lookup and manual typing). Frontline copilots with mandatory human sign-off capture 80% of speed gains while insulating the bank from liability. | `04_FlagshipA/prioritisation/use_case_prioritisation.md` |
| **Q9** | **How does the DPDP Act 2023 change your solution design?** | M6 (Flagship A) | By enforcing three non-negotiable architectural safeguards: 1. **Client-side PII masking gateway** before any text leaves the bank perimeter (944 intercepts in our pilot); 2. **Enterprise Data Processing Agreements (DPA)** with cloud LLM providers guaranteeing Zero Data Retention (ZDR) and India data residency (Mumbai region); 3. **Audited 72-hour breach notification runbook** and cryptographic isolation (TLS 1.3 / AES-256) of prompt logs. | `04_FlagshipA/governance/governance_plan.md` (Control Map) |
