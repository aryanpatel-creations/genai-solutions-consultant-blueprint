# 90-Second STAR Interview Story (star_interview_story.md)

> **Context:** Behavioral & Case Study Interview Response for Big 4 Advisory / Strategy Consulting roles.  
> **Target Question:** *"Tell me about a complex project where you leveraged analytics and Generative AI to solve an enterprise business problem."*  
> **Duration:** Exactly 90 Seconds (~220 Words)  
> **Pacing:** Calm, structured, confident, executive presence.

---

### Situation (20 Seconds)
*"At Kestrel Bank, a mid-sized commercial bank handling 40,000 retail complaints a month across 120 agents, rising dispute volumes were straining operations. Average handle time reached 25 minutes, with agents spending 60% of their time manually querying disconnected core systems and PDF circulars. Worse, 12% of complaints drifted past Day 25, risking mandatory 30-day escalation to the RBI Ombudsman at ₹3,500 in direct fees per case."*

### Task (15 Seconds)
*"As the lead techno-functional consultant, my mandate was to diagnose operational bottlenecks, evaluate where Generative AI could legitimately add value, build the financial business case, and design an end-to-end, regulatorily compliant transformation roadmap."*

### Action (35 Seconds)
*"I approached this through a structured consulting methodology:
First, I ran a Lean Six Sigma DMAIC diagnostic in Python on 346,000 complaints, proving that 15% were petty fee disputes solvable via a simple ₹1,500 frontline discretionary waiver—eliminating waste without AI.
Next, using the AI Decision Ladder, I scoped three targeted frontline copilots—triage, policy RAG, and response drafting—while deliberately rejecting autonomous agents to prevent hallucination liability under RBI FREE-AI rules.
I built a 3-year DCF financial model in Excel demonstrating that while hard labor savings were negative (-₹23.4L NPV), avoided Ombudsman escalations delivered a +₹127.5 Lakh Full NPV with an 18-month payback.
Finally, I built a 16-KPI Streamlit dashboard with 22 passing unit tests to give leadership real-time telemetry."*

### Result (20 Seconds)
*"The blueprint delivered a 24% reduction in handling time, cut 540 Ombudsman escalations, and proved that frontline adoption—not token cost—is the primary financial lever, driving a ₹174 Lakh NPV swing. This project established that successful AI strategy starts with process redesign, not model hype."*
