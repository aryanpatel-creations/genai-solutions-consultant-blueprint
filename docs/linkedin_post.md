### Post Content

"I scoped the AI opportunity like a consultant, then built the prototype like an engineer."

Most enterprise GenAI initiatives stall because they start with the model rather than the process. 

For my latest portfolio project, I served as lead techno-functional consultant designing the **AI Transformation Blueprint for Kestrel Bank** (a mid-sized commercial bank handling ~40,000 monthly complaints under RBI RB-IOS guidelines).

Here are 3 counter-intuitive takeaways from our diagnostic and financial modeling:

1️⃣ **Start with Lean, not LLMs:**  
Our Lean Six Sigma DMAIC analysis across 346,481 bank complaints revealed that 15% of disputes were petty fee waiver claims trapped in 48-hour approval queues. Granting frontline agents immediate discretionary waiver authority up to ₹1,500 eliminated thousands of rework loops—before writing a single prompt.

2️⃣ **Labor savings alone won't fund your build:**  
Applying a conservative 50% labor realization factor, cashable agent hours yielded a **negative Net Present Value (-₹23.4 Lakh)**. The business case became viable (**+₹127.5 Lakh NPV, 18-month payback, 86% ROI**) only when factoring in statutory risk mitigation: avoiding 540 Ombudsman conciliations (+₹194.4 Lakh saved).

3️⃣ **Adoption is your #1 financial lever (not token pricing):**  
Variable model tokens represented just **8.2% of our 3-year TCO** (₹2.12 / complaint). But our Tornado sensitivity analysis proved that frontline adoption creates a massive **₹174.1 Lakh NPV swing**. Change management, not model optimization, drives the P&L.

To safeguard compliance under RBI FREE-AI and the DPDP Act 2023, our To-Be BPMN architecture strictly enforces Human-in-the-Loop review for 100% of outgoing customer communications—autonomous agents were deliberately rejected.
